01 / The mission

Beyond the final signal.

Across the Moon and Mars, robotic explorers have expanded our understanding of worlds beyond Earth. Some completed their journeys after years of discovery. Others changed what we believe is possible.

Echoes of Exploration is an interactive digital archive dedicated to remembering these remarkable machines and the knowledge they left behind.

"Every journey leaves a trace. Every discovery becomes part of our story." THE EVENSTAR VISION

03 / The journey

A timeline of discovery.

Milestones that changed the history of robotic and human exploration.

JULY 1971

The Moon gets its first rover

Apollo 15 makes the Lunar Roving Vehicle's first journey across the lunar surface.

JANUARY 2004

Opportunity arrives on Mars

NASA's Mars Exploration Rover begins a mission designed to last just 90 sols.

APRIL 2021

Ingenuity takes flight

Humanity achieves its first powered, controlled flight on another planet.

JUNE 2018

Opportunity's last communication

A planet-wide dust storm interrupts communication with the long-running rover.

JANUARY 2024

Ingenuity completes its mission

After 72 flights, the pioneering Mars helicopter's mission comes to an end.

04 / About the project

Remembering the machines
that took us further.

Created by Team Evenstar, Echoes of Exploration celebrates the achievements of space exploration through storytelling, educational content and an accessible digital archive.

Our vision

To inspire curiosity about space exploration and make the achievements of robotic explorers accessible to everyone. We believe a mission's legacy should continue long after its final transmission.
